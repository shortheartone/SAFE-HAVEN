**Date**: August 2026  
**Vision Period**: 2026–2029 (3 years)  
**Status**: Ready for community governance vote

## What We're Building

SAFE-HAVEN is a **trustless, community-governed vault protocol on Stellar** that enables secure time-locked deposits with optional early-exit penalties. Unlike traditional DeFi protocols, SAFE-HAVEN commits to permanent admin renunciation, meaning the protocol belongs to the community forever—not to founders.

---

## Mission Statement

**Empower users and organizations to securely lock and manage digital assets on the Stellar blockchain, with trustless, community-governed infrastructure that prioritizes transparency, security, and long-term sustainability.**

## Governance Model

SAFE-HAVEN governance is **on-chain, trustless, and time-locked**:

1. **Propose**: Anyone can propose a contract upgrade with evidence (diff, audit)
2. **Review**: Independent reviewer verifies the audit and code
3. **Vote**: Community votes (minimum 30% participation)
4. **Timelock**: 14-day delay before execution (allows veto window)
5. **Veto**: Any steward can veto (prevents execution)
6. **Execute**: Anyone can execute after timelock expires

**Result**: No single person can unilaterally upgrade the contract.

## What We're NOT Doing

This vision deliberately excludes:

- **Financial projections** — We don't predict TVL, token prices, or market adoption
- **Detailed technical roadmap** — That's for quarterly planning, not strategic vision
- **Guaranteeing success** — Markets are unpredictable; we commit to effort, not outcomes
- **Founder immortalization** — The protocol should survive without any original founders
- **Centralized decision-making** — No small group controls the protocol
